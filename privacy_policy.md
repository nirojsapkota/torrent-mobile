# Privacy Policy for openTorrent

**Last updated: September 2, 2026**

openTorrent ("we", "our", or "the App") is a free, open-source Android application that allows users to download and manage torrent files.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how the App handles information.

## 1. Information We Collect

openTorrent does not intentionally collect, store, or sell personal information.

The App may access certain information on your device only when necessary to provide its functionality, including:

* Torrent files or magnet links that you choose to open or use.
* Files and folders selected by you as download locations.
* Network information required to connect to peers and download torrent content.
* Device storage required to save downloaded files.

We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information to use the App.

## 2. Torrent and Network Information

When you use a BitTorrent application, your device may communicate with other BitTorrent peers over the internet.

As part of the BitTorrent protocol, your IP address may be visible to other peers participating in the same torrent swarm. This communication is necessary for the BitTorrent protocol to function and is not information that openTorrent independently collects or stores.

openTorrent does not operate or control the torrent trackers, peer-to-peer networks, or content that users access through the App.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of torrents complies with applicable laws and regulations.

## 3. Files and Storage

openTorrent may request access to device storage or use Android's file access mechanisms so that you can:

* Select torrent files.
* Select download locations.
* Save downloaded content.
* Manage files downloaded through the App.

The App does not upload your personal files to us.

Downloaded content is stored on your device or in a location selected by you.

## 4. Personal Information

We do not intentionally collect or store personal information such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Postal address
* Contacts
* Photos or personal documents
* Account passwords
* Payment information

## 5. Third-Party Services

openTorrent may communicate with third-party services when required for functionality such as torrent trackers, peer discovery, or other network operations.

These third-party services operate independently from openTorrent and may process information according to their own privacy policies.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party services you use through the App.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

We do not intentionally share personal information with third parties.

Information exchanged through the BitTorrent protocol, including your IP address, may be visible to other peers as an inherent part of peer-to-peer communication.

## 7. Data Security

Because openTorrent does not maintain a centralized account or personal-information database, there is no openTorrent user account database containing your personal information.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should take appropriate precautions when downloading or sharing files over the internet.

## 8. Children's Privacy

openTorrent is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Your Responsibilities

openTorrent is a tool for peer-to-peer file transfers. Users are responsible for:

* The torrent files and magnet links they use.
* The content they download or share.
* Complying with copyright laws and other applicable laws.
* Ensuring that they have the appropriate rights or permissions to download or distribute content.

We do not control or endorse content distributed through the BitTorrent network.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. Continued use of openTorrent after changes are made constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
